What is ThinkNeuralAI Platform?

ThinkNeuralAI is a suite of enterprise AI agents designed to automate business processes, analyze visual data, and enable intelligent decision-making. Our product ecosystem includes:

  • Sakshi.ai. Visual AI for analysing CCTV feeds, headcount, compliance, and operational insights.
  • SpaceZap.ai. AI-driven outlet and store design automation using mobile scans and automated layout generation.
  • DataDime.ai. A private enterprise GPT that lets users chat with their data, generate insights, automate reports, and forecast business outcomes.
