ThinkNeural at In‑Store Asia, Mumbai.

Bombay Exhibition Centre, MumbaiStand F42Add dates

In-Store Asia is the largest trade fair in the Indian subcontinent for store design, visual merchandising and retail technology. Every year it brings retail and brand teams to Mumbai to see what comes next for the store.

We were at stand F42 with one message. From site to store in hours, not weeks.

Visitors watched SpaceZap at work. Scan a raw space. Automate the design. Get the RECCE, ten technical layouts and the BOQ on the spot. The rest of the ThinkNeural ecosystem came along too. Sakshi for what happens on the floor once the store opens. DataDime for the questions that follow.

We also took the stage at the In-Store Asia conference. Our team joined a panel on fit-outs and infrastructure for new retail projects. It was a good room to make the case that store rollouts can move at the speed of software.

Thank you to everyone who stopped by the stand and the session. If we met at the show, let's keep the conversation going.
