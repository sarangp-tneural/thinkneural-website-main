Secu Fest Expo 2026 is Kerala's largest security and automation expo. It is organised by AKESSIA, the All Kinds of Electronic Security and System Integrators Association. That is exactly the room Sakshi was built for.

ThinkNeural came on board as Platinum Sponsor and Platinum Technology Partner, with Sakshi AI at stall P3.

Our message was simple. Your CCTV sees everything. Does it tell you anything? Sakshi turns any camera into real-time alerts, insights and compliance. No new hardware.

At the stall we walked visitors through live alerts and dashboards. We talked through use cases across retail and consumer electronics, BFSI and gold loan, education, manufacturing and pharma, and salons and wellness.

On stage, AKESSIA honoured Sakshi AI with a memento. Thank you to the AKESSIA team for a well-run expo.

Integrators are how Sakshi reaches every store, bank and campus in Kerala. If you install cameras, let's talk about partnering.
