Huddle Global is India's largest beachside startup festival. Kerala Startup Mission runs it on the seafront at Kovalam. The 2025 edition was its seventh.

ThinkNeural was selected for the festival's Product Launch track. Our team took the main stage and launched our product to a room of founders, investors and industry leaders.

Three days by the sea also meant three days of conversations. Investors, mentors, fellow founders and future customers, all in one place.

Thank you to the KSUM team for building a stage like this for Kerala startups.
