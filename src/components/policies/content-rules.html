Content Rules

Last Updated: November 8, 2026

1. Introduction

These Content Rules set out what you may and may not do with the ThinkNeural.ai website and products, including Sakshi.ai, SpaceZap.ai and DataDime.ai (the “Services”). They apply to everyone who uses the Services and form part of our Platform Licence Agreement. Our Services work with camera feeds, store layouts, business data and AI-generated answers, so these rules are written with that in mind.

2. What These Rules Cover

“Content” means anything you upload, connect, enter or generate through the Services. That includes video and images from cameras, floor plans and scans, documents and datasets, messages and prompts, and the outputs and reports the Services produce for you.

3. General Rules

You must not use the Services to upload, store, share or generate content that:

  • is unlawful, or promotes or assists any unlawful activity;
  • infringes anyone’s copyright, trademark, trade secret or other rights (see our Copyright Policy);
  • is defamatory, harassing, threatening, hateful or discriminatory;
  • is obscene, sexually explicit or exploits or endangers minors;
  • is false or misleading in a way intended to deceive, including impersonating a person or organisation;
  • contains malware or is designed to disrupt, damage or gain unauthorised access to systems or data.

4. Cameras and Video Analytics

If you connect cameras or video to Sakshi.ai or similar features, you are responsible for using them lawfully. You must:

  • have the legal right to monitor the space and to process the footage;
  • place cameras only in suitable areas, and never in places where people expect privacy, such as restrooms, changing rooms or trial rooms;
  • display clear notices that cameras and analytics are in use, as required by law and your own policies;
  • use analytics only for stated, lawful purposes, such as safety, compliance, headcount and operations;
  • not use the Services for covert tracking or to identify, profile or target individuals unlawfully;
  • respect your obligations under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable law, including data retention and access requests.

5. Responsible Use of AI

  • Do not use AI outputs to create deceptive or manipulated content, such as deepfakes, intended to mislead people.
  • Do not use the Services to discriminate unfairly against people or to make significant decisions about them, for example about employment or access to services, without appropriate human review.
  • Check AI-generated answers, insights and designs before relying on them. They may contain errors.
  • Do not attempt to extract, copy or reverse engineer our models, prompts or training data, or to manipulate the Services into ignoring their safeguards.

6. Data You Connect

Only connect data you are allowed to use. Do not upload sensitive information, such as government ID numbers, health records or financial account details, unless it is needed for your use case, you have the right to process it, and you have agreed this with us in writing.

7. Security and Fair Use

  • Keep your login details confidential and tell us promptly if you suspect misuse.
  • Do not probe, scan or test the security of the Services without our written permission.
  • Do not overload or interfere with the Services or bypass usage limits or access controls.

8. Monitoring and Enforcement

We do not routinely review your content, but we may investigate if we receive a complaint, suspect a breach of these rules or are required to by law. If content or use breaks these rules, we may, depending on seriousness:

  • ask you to fix it or remove it;
  • remove or disable the content or restrict features;
  • suspend or end your access;
  • report the matter to the relevant authorities and cooperate with lawful requests.

Where practical, we will tell you what happened and why.

9. Reporting a Concern

If you see content or use of the Services that breaks these rules, or you believe a camera or analytics deployment affects you unlawfully, please tell us at the address below. Include as much detail as you can so we can look into it.

10. Changes to These Rules

We may update these Content Rules as our Services and the law change. The updated version will be posted here with a new “Last Updated” date. Continued use of the Services means you accept the updated rules.
