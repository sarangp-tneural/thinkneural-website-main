1. Introduction

This Cookies Policy explains how ThinkNeural.ai (“we”, “us”, “our”) uses cookies and similar technologies on our website (www.thinkneural.ai). It should be read together with our Privacy Policy.

2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by a website. Similar technologies, such as browser local storage and session storage, store small pieces of data in your browser. They are commonly used to make websites work, remember your choices and understand how a site is used.

3. What We Use

We keep our use of these technologies to a minimum. At the time of the “Last Updated” date above, our website does not use advertising cookies, cross-site tracking or third-party analytics cookies.

a. Essential and Functional Storage

  • Session storage: A small flag stored in your browser for the length of your visit so that the loading screen is not repeated every time you move between pages. It is deleted when you close the tab or browser and contains no personal data.

b. Third-Party Services

Some features load resources from third parties. These providers may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser type when your browser requests the resource, and may set their own cookies or similar identifiers under their own policies.

  • Google Fonts: Used to display the fonts on our website. Fonts are requested from Google’s servers.
  • EmailJS: Used to deliver messages you submit through our contact form. Your form details are sent to EmailJS only when you submit the form.
  • FAQ Assistant: The chat widget is loaded from a hosted service in an embedded frame once you open it. If you use it, what you type is processed by that service to produce answers. If you use voice input, your browser will ask for permission to use your microphone.

4. Managing Cookies

You can control or delete cookies and stored data through your browser settings. Most browsers let you block all cookies, block third-party cookies only, or clear stored data when you close the browser. Blocking essential storage will not stop the website from working, but some features may behave differently, for example the loading screen may appear more than once.

5. Changes to Our Use of Cookies

If we add analytics, advertising or other non-essential cookies in future, we will update this policy and, where required by law, ask for your consent before they are set. We may also update this policy to reflect legal or technical changes. The “Last Updated” date above shows when it last changed.
